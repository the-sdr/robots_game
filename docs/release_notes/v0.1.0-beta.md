First playable build of **Robots**: a small tracked robot wakes in the remains
of a failed civilization that nature has reclaimed. About 20 minutes of play:
the house, the forest, and the first district of the ruined city.

**Download** `Robots_Beta_v0.1.0-beta_Windows.exe` below and run it. One file,
no installer, 64-bit Windows.

- Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher (the exe isn't signed):
  click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- Needs a graphics driver with Vulkan support (Intel UHD, AMD or NVIDIA from the
  last ~8 years with current drivers).

**Controls**: WASD drive, mouse look, scroll zoom, E use/dock, Tab parts and
builds, click use tool, Q next tool, Esc pause.

**Helping us test**: F2 shows your position and FPS. F3 saves your position and
F4 logs 5 seconds of performance; both go to
`%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Robots Beta\playtest\`. Send `saves.md` and
`perf.md` with a few lines on what felt wrong.

This is a beta: placeholder art and story, and a lot left to build.
